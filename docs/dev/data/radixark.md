## November 21, 2026 audit

DaisyRose 27B was released on November 18 as Apache-2.0 open weights under the
`RadixArk/DaisyRose-27B` repository and local serving selector. The model card
establishes text/image input, text output, reasoning, tool-call parsing, and a
262,144-token context window. OpenRouter serves it as
`radixark/daisyrose-27b`.
